# Privacy Policy — DockMobile

_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

DockMobile is an independent, open-source Android client for Dockhand. It
does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app does

- You enter the address of your own Dockhand server and a personal API
  token. These are stored **locally on your device** using Android's
  encrypted storage (Android Keystore via expo-secure-store).
- All communication goes **directly** between your phone and the server you
  configured. No third party, analytics provider, or cloud service is
  involved.
- The app does not use advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No account or profile data.
- No usage analytics or telemetry.
- No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
- No data is sent to the developer or any third party.

## Your control

- "Remove connection" in Settings deletes the stored server address and
  token from your device.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
